# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owner-led New Zealand businesses, roughly 20-200 staff, in industries with a field/back-office split: construction, logistics, recruitment, healthcare, professional services, trades, plus finance. The visitor is a director, GM, or ops lead who has been reading about AI for months, feels behind, and distrusts hype. They arrive from search, an AI assistant's citation, or a founder's LinkedIn post, often on a phone after hours. Their job to be done: work out whether AI applies to *their* operation and whether these two people can be trusted to deploy it. The action that matters: book a Discovery Call.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Engine AI Consulting Limited (engineai.co.nz): a single-page scrolling home plus four product sub-pages (/claude, /copilot, /openclaw, /builds). Primary purpose: convert curious NZ business owners into Discovery Call bookings (contact form → sales@engineai.co.nz). Secondary purpose: be discoverable and citable by AI search systems (GEO) — the site must practise the AI-orchestration competence it sells. Success looks like booked discovery calls and AI/search visibility for product-specific queries (Claude Co-Work, Copilot Studio, OpenClaw).

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, minimal. Calm authority: we've done the work, here's how we can help. Operator language, not consultant language ("Two operators. Not two consultants."). Direct NZ English, short sentences, whitespace does the heavy lifting. Premium dark-room feel: near-black canvas, warm off-white text, one gold accent spent sparingly. Dry, grounded, zero hype.

## Anti-references

- Big-consultancy corporate sites (Deloitte/Accenture-style stock-photo grids, jargon walls)
- Startup-bro SaaS landing pages: gradient text, hero-metric templates, purple-neon "AI" aesthetics, robot imagery
- AI-guru course-seller pages: urgency banners, countdown timers, exclamation marks
- Light-theme corporate minimalism — this site is dark throughout, no light sections
- Stock photography as decoration; placeholder imagery of any kind
- Banned vocabulary: "leverage", "synergy", "digital transformation"

## Design Principles

1. **Let the words do the work.** Copy is locked and final; design's job is to give it authority. Big light-weight type, generous whitespace, minimal ornamentation.
2. **Practise what we preach.** The site itself is proof of competence: fast (95+ Lighthouse target), precise, GEO-optimised, lean. A slow or sloppy page contradicts the pitch.
3. **Forward momentum.** Every section creates pull toward "Book a Discovery Call". No dead ends, no detours that don't build trust.
4. **Grounded over glossy.** Real founders, real work, real constraints. No invented numbers, no stock-photo gloss, nothing that couldn't survive a sceptical operator's squint.
5. **One accent, spent sparingly.** Gold is the only raised voice. Product accents (Copilot blue, OpenClaw teal, Builds green) live only on their own sub-pages, never mixed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort; no formal WCAG conformance claim. Keep what's shipped (skip-to-content link, aria-labels, semantic landmarks) and never regress it. Known gaps tracked as backlog, not blockers: no `prefers-reduced-motion` handling for hero/scroll animations; shipped `#888888` secondary text sits below 4.5:1 on near-black (the canonical token `#ACA69B` resolves this when adopted).
