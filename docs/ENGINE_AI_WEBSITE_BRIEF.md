## SECTION 2: THE PROBLEM (Why this matters)

**Headline:** There's a massive gap between what AI can do and what businesses are actually doing with it.

**Body copy:**

68% of New Zealand SMEs have no plans to even evaluate AI. Not implement — evaluate. Meanwhile, 75% of CEOs say AI has had little or no impact on their business, but their number one fear is falling behind in it.

That's not a technology problem. It's a translation problem.

The technology exists. The capability is proven. What's missing is someone who can walk into a business of 30 or 50 or 100 people, understand how they actually operate, and show them — concretely, with numbers — where AI creates real value.

## SECTION 5: HOW WE WORK (The Model)

**Section tag:** The Model
**Headline:** Discover. Build. Orchestrate. Repeat.

**Step 1 — Discover**
A deep, structured assessment of how your business actually operates, where AI creates measurable value, and what to do first. Process mapping, stakeholder interviews, systems audits, and a prioritised roadmap backed by ROI estimates. Not a survey. Not a workshop.

**Step 2 — Build**
We deploy the highest-impact opportunities as working AI agents integrated into your existing systems. Not demos. Not proofs of concept. Production systems that do real work from day one.

**Step 3 — Orchestrate**
We stay on as an ongoing partner to monitor, tune, expand, and evolve. AI isn't a project you finish. It's a capability you compound. Every month, the agents get better, new opportunities emerge, and the gap between you and your competitors widens.

**Callout:** Projects land clients. Retainers build the business. That's our economic model — and it's also the truth about AI adoption. The value compounds over time.

**Design notes:** Three-step horizontal flow or vertical timeline. Each step gets an icon (magnifying glass / wrench / dashboard). Gold accent line connecting them.

## SECTION 6: PRINCIPLES

**Section tag:** What We Believe
**Headline:** Our principles aren't aspirational. They're operational.

1. **Go where the pain is** — We look for where the business is bleeding — time, money, quality, sanity. AI is a painkiller, not a vitamin.

2. **Ship, then iterate** — A working agent in production teaches you more than six months of planning. We deploy early, measure ruthlessly, and improve in motion.

3. **Be honest about what AI can't do** — Our credibility is built on what we don't recommend as much as what we do. If a process isn't a good fit for AI, we say so.

4. **Augment humans, don't replace them** — We build AI that makes a team of 10 perform like a team of 25, not AI that turns a team of 10 into a team of 3.

5. **Build the relationship, not the dependency** — We don't build black boxes. We build systems the client understands, document everything, and train the team to own what we've built.

6. **Senior people on every engagement** — Two-person boutique means no bench. No juniors learning on your dime. When you engage Engine AI, you get the founders. Every call. Every build. Every decision.

7. **Prove it with our own work** — We don't recommend tools we haven't used. Every methodology we bring to a client has been tested on our own projects first. We eat our own cooking.

**Design notes:** Cards with left-border accent colours (rotate Gold, Teal, Green, etc). Numbered. Brief — one or two sentences max visible, expand on hover/click if needed.

## TECHNICAL NOTES FOR BUILD

- Dark theme throughout — matches brand and thesis doc aesthetic
- Smooth scroll with anchor navigation

- Mobile-first responsive — single column on mobile, expand on desktop
- No heavy frameworks needed — Next.js static export or even plain HTML + Tailwind

## CONTENT TONE GUIDE

- **Voice:** Direct, confident, grounded. Not corporate. Not startup-bro.
- **Perspective:** "We" (Engine AI). Address the reader as "you" / "your business".
- **Avoid:** Jargon, buzzwords, "leverage", "synergy", "digital transformation". Say what you mean plainly.
- **Energy:** Calm authority. We've done the work. We know what works. Here's how we can help.
- **Length:** Keep paragraphs short. Max 3-4 sentences. Let whitespace do the heavy lifting.
